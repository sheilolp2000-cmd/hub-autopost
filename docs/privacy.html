Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Hub Autopost is a personal tool operated by a single creator to publish videos to their own TikTok accounts.

Data we access

When you connect a TikTok account, we receive an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) through TikTok Login Kit. We use them only to publish videos you chose and to show which account is connected.

Storage

Access tokens are stored encrypted as secrets in a private automation environment. We do not store any other TikTok user data, and we do not access content, followers or messages.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

Deletion

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Apps and services. Stored tokens are then deleted on request.
