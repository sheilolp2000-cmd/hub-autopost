Hub Autopost

Hub Autopost is a private publishing tool. It lets a single creator schedule and publish their own short videos to their own TikTok accounts. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

How it works: the creator connects their TikTok account with TikTok Login, picks finished videos and a time, and the tool uploads each video to that account at the scheduled time using the TikTok Content Posting API.
